# Example: low-stakes decision where the panel genuinely agrees

**Prompt:** Should we switch our card payment processor to a cheaper provider?

**Placeholders supplied when asked:** current provider charges 2.75% flat, monthly card turnover ~R120,000, 2 till points, staff would need a short walkthrough on a new card machine.

---

**Active lenses:** the core four, plus CFO. Not HR, retraining two till operators on a new card machine for under an hour doesn't rise to a real staffing decision. Not Marketing, customers don't know or care which processor runs behind the till.

**Quick answer:** Yes, switching saves roughly R1,205/month (about 36%) at current turnover, with low operational risk. Confidence 4/5.

## Evidence Snapshot

**From you:**
- Current provider: 2.75% flat rate
- Monthly card turnover: ~R120,000
- 2 till points, short device walkthrough needed for staff

**From research:**
- Yoco's published Core plan pricing (R0 monthly fee): 2.30% up to R50k, 1.35% on the R50k-R200k band. Sourced directly from Yoco's own pricing page.
- Gap: didn't verify the current provider's contract terms or whether an early-termination cost applies, and didn't compare against every other South African provider since Yoco was the one specifically being considered.

## Panel Verdicts

**CEO (strategy):** Support. A positive-margin operational efficiency improvement with low strategic risk, doesn't touch what the business sells or how it's positioned.

**COO (execution):** Conditional support. A brief reconciliation window while switching card machines, but for two till points this is small and contained.

**PM (delivery):** Support, scoped. Recommends a defined cutover date, a brief staff walkthrough beforehand, and keeping the old facility active through its notice period as a fallback.

**Founder (speed):** Support. Low-risk, quick win, the main cost is a short admin task.

**CFO (numbers):** Support, with the math. Current cost at 2.75% on R120,000 monthly turnover is R3,300/month. Yoco's Core plan works out to R2,095/month at this turnover (R1,150 on the first R50k, R945 on the remaining R70k). That's roughly R1,205/month saved, about R14,460/year, against effectively no cost to switch.

## Cross-Examination

All four core roles and CFO agree here, genuinely, no real contradiction surfaced. The one live risk (COO's brief reconciliation window) is fully addressed by PM's cutover plan, so nothing here is load-bearing enough to lower confidence much. Worth stating plainly rather than manufacturing a disagreement that isn't there.

## Final Call

**Recommendation:** Yes, switch, using a defined cutover date and keeping the current facility active through its notice period as a fallback.

**Confidence:** 4/5, not 5, the saving is based on today's turnover mix and Yoco's published Core-plan rates, not a full comparison against every other provider or confirmation there's no early-termination cost with the current one.

**Why:** the fee gap is real and sourced directly from the new provider's own pricing, the operational lift is small and well contained by a simple cutover plan, and every role agrees without needing to be talked into it.

**What would flip this:** if the current provider has an early-termination penalty larger than a few months of the saving, or if actual turnover is meaningfully different from what was used here.
