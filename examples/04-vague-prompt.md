# Example: vague prompt, correctly refuses to guess

**Prompt:** Should we grow the business?

---

**Response:**

"Grow the business" could mean a lot of different things with very different risk profiles, opening a new location, adding a product line, hiring more staff, increasing marketing spend, extending hours, entering a new market. Which of these, or something else, is actually on the table?

---

No panel is run, no lenses are activated, and no research or evidence gathering happens. This is the intended behavior: a vague decision produces a vague, unusable evaluation no matter how good the panel is, so the skill stops at Step 1 and asks rather than inventing a specific decision to evaluate on the user's behalf.
